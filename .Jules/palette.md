## 2024-04-14 - Accessible Icon-Only Delete Buttons
**Learning:** Found an accessibility issue pattern where `opacity-0 group-hover:opacity-100` utility classes were repeatedly used for hover-revealed delete action buttons (like the `Trash2` icons in `CropPhotoGallery.tsx` and `TaskNotes.tsx`), making the actions entirely invisible to keyboard users since they lacked `focus` states, tooltips, and ARIA labels.
**Action:** Always pair `group-hover:opacity-100` with `focus:opacity-100` on interactive elements and `focus-within:opacity-100` on their parent containers to ensure keyboard accessibility. When adding tooltips to shadcn icon-only buttons, use `aria-hidden="true"` on the svg, provide a screen-reader friendly `aria-label` on the button itself, and ensure the button uses `asChild` semantics when triggering a `Tooltip`.
