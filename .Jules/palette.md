## 2024-05-15 - Icon Button Accessibility
**Learning:** Icon-only buttons (like delete buttons or mobile menu buttons) often lack aria-labels, making them inaccessible to screen readers.
**Action:** Add aria-labels to icon-only buttons for better accessibility.
## 2024-05-15 - Icon Button Accessibility
**Learning:** Icon-only buttons (like delete buttons or mobile menu buttons) often lack aria-labels and proper keyboard focus visibility, making them inaccessible.
**Action:** Add aria-labels to icon-only buttons directly, hide decorative SVGs with aria-hidden, and ensure hover-revealed buttons are focusable via keyboard.
